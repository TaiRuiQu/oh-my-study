The mobile interface embeds a WOFF2 subset of Noto Sans SC, sourced from the Google Fonts repository at https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc and licensed under the SIL Open Font License in OFL.txt.

The subset contains the English and Simplified Chinese localization catalog characters and printable ASCII. It retains the variable weight axis and OpenType layout features. Japanese and Korean interfaces use their platform UI fonts. The font is served directly by the local receiver as embedded data; opening the phone interface does not contact a font service.

Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/notosanssc/NotoSansSC%5Bwght%5D.ttf

Source SHA-256: A3041811A78C361B1DE50F953C805E0244951C21C5BD412F7232EF0D899AF0DA.

Subset produced with FontTools 4.67.0 and Brotli 1.2.1. Input glyph text is the concatenated en-US.xml and zh-CN.xml catalogs plus U+0020–U+007E. Format is WOFF2; all layout features are retained.
