# Oh My Study document restoration models

The bundled geometry.onnx and illumination.onnx are project fine-tuned models, finalized on 2026-10-27. Together they contain 64,997,044 bytes of FP32 ONNX data. Inference is local with ONNX Runtime CPU; Python, CUDA and OpenCV are not required.

Geometry starts from UVDoc by Tanguy Magne and coauthors, pinned at 4c9b82b537057aff2526e6dd118a847cdd072e82, under the accompanying UVDoc-LICENSE.txt. Its full-frame RGB input is [1,3,712,488], and its normalized backward grid is [1,2,45,31]. It is fine-tuned on automatically matched paired document views. The entire input frame reaches the model before the single output remap; there is no four-corner perspective preprocessing.

Illumination starts from SyntheticDoc by Daniel Woortmann, Tanguy Magne and Olga Sorkine-Hornung, pinned at aabd9cf05f479781df432e3bd7e940f3e6716dbd, under the accompanying SyntheticDoc-LICENSE.txt. Its geometry head is removed. Its [1,3,512,512] RGB input produces a [1,3,256,256] RGB illumination divisor after 2,000 adaptation updates on 300 RealDAE color/shadow training pairs, a fixed 1,536-pair SD7K subset, synthetic lighting and identity examples. Training data is not distributed with the app.

TrainedDocumentRestoration.cs implements input resizing, normalized-grid interpolation with align_corners=True, one full-resolution geometry remap, illumination division, color protection, neutral-ink correction and continuous scan levels. Neural output alone is not a restored document. Both input tensors use RGB float32 [0,1]. Geometry output preserves the upright source photograph's dimensions and aspect; the model does not predict the physical paper aspect. EXIF rotation, automatic orientation and manual rotation remain available. The unmodified PaddleOCR PP-LCNet_x1_0_doc_ori classifier is retained before geometry inference under Orientation-LICENSE.txt (Apache-2.0), pinned at Hugging Face revision 7330ab7039123e46af2dc03154b9969aa412c61d. Its 6,788,069 bytes are additional to the two fine-tuned restoration models. Its classifier-only center crop does not crop the image passed to geometry. Explicit manual rotation takes precedence over automatic orientation.

Fine-tuning does not establish universal quality or latency guarantees. Hard shadow boundaries, mixed-light color casts, faint strokes, bleed-through and blur remain limitations. This pipeline performs no character synthesis or learned deblurring. The source hash, artifact hashes, shapes and byte counts are pinned in packaging/restoration.lock.json and are checked during asset preparation. Preserve the upstream attribution and license files with redistributed model files. Dataset terms must be reviewed independently for any further use or redistribution of training materials.
